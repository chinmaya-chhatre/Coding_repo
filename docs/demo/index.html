Webhook signature lab

Every provider signs its webhooks differently, and most “signature mismatch” tickets come down to one detail: the exact bytes, the exact URL or the clock. Pick a provider to see a real-shaped request and how it is signed. This runs entirely in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere.

The sample payloads are illustrative test data, not recordings of real traffic, and the secrets are public demo values. The verifier behind this page is HookScope, an open-source webhook receiver that verifies, stores, forwards and replays these deliveries.
